Batting score weights

Read these results with care

  • Pressure is an approximation. It ignores target, required run rate, match importance and opposition strength.
  • Dot balls are approximate. Byes, leg-byes and other extras cannot be separated reliably.
  • Samples differ. A player with 120 balls and one with 600 are not equally well supported. Rishabh Pant (122 balls) sits just above the minimum.
  • Weights shape the order. Different weights could change the ranking.
  • Not career rankings. They cover only the supplied dataset and do not predict future form.
